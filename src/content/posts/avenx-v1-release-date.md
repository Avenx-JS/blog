We are excited to announce that the release date for **Avenx.js v1.0.0 is officially set for October 31st, 2026! 🚀**

We are now entering the final phase of development. Over the coming weeks, our focus will be on finishing the remaining features, improving stability, and polishing the overall developer experience to make v1.0.0 the best release possible.

Before the release, we want to complete the final planned features and ensure that everything is ready for a stable and reliable first major version.

📌 **Important milestone: Feature Freeze @ October 15th, 2026**

On October 15th, we will enter **feature freeze**. After this date, no new features will be added to v1.0.0. The remaining time will be dedicated entirely to:

* 🐛 Bug fixes
* 🧪 Testing and quality improvements
* 📚 Documentation updates
* ⚡ Performance optimizations
* 🔒 Stability improvements

A huge thank you to everyone who has contributed, tested, reported issues, shared feedback, or supported the project. The growth of Avenx.js has only been possible because of the amazing community around it.

We are incredibly excited for this milestone and can't wait to release the first stable version of Avenx.js.

Save the dates:

* 🚀 **October 15th, 2026 - Feature Freeze**
* 🎃 **October 31st, 2026 - Avenx.js v1.0.0 Release**

See you at v1.0.0!

The Avenx.js Team
