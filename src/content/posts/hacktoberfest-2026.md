Starting October 1st, we're bringing back a little tradition from previous years: **throughout October, we'll create and label issues specifically for Hacktoberfest contributors.**

Whether you want to fix a small bug, improve the documentation, work on tooling or tackle something more challenging, there will be issues to pick from.

This year is also the first Hacktoberfest under the new program, so it felt like the right moment to bring the tradition back — and to give new contributors a way into Avenx.js.

## How to take part

1. Browse the issues labelled [`hacktoberfest`](https://github.com/Avenx-JS/avenx-js/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Ahacktoberfest).
2. Read the [contributing guide](https://github.com/Avenx-JS/avenx-js/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) before you start — pull requests need to follow it, including a proper description.
3. Open your pull request against `main` and reference the issue it closes.

If you've been looking for an open-source project to contribute to this October, keep an eye on our issues.
