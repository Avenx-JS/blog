After careful consideration, we've decided to postpone the planned Avenx.js v1.0.0 release.

**The new release date is January 31, 2027.**

This wasn't an easy decision, but we believe it's the right one for the project.

## Why we're waiting

Avenx.js has grown significantly over the past months. What started as a relatively small project has evolved into an entire ecosystem, with a growing community, more contributors and an increasingly ambitious vision.

With that growth comes responsibility. We don't want to rush the first stable release just to meet a deadline. Instead, we want enough time to properly review, test, refine and stabilize everything, so that 1.0 works as reliably as possible, offers a great developer experience and reflects the standards we want Avenx.js to stand for.

> We don't just want to release something that works. We want to release something we can genuinely be proud of.

## What happens until then

Between now and January 31, our focus is on:

- resolving remaining issues and edge cases
- improving stability and reliability across the ecosystem
- thoroughly testing core functionality and tooling
- refining the developer experience
- getting the documentation and release infrastructure ready

In the meantime, the [release candidate](/avenx-1-0-0-rc-1) remains the best way to try what's coming — and every bug you report now is one that won't ship in 1.0.

We'd rather take a few additional months and release something truly solid than rush something that doesn't meet our own expectations.

Thank you to everyone who has contributed, reported issues, tested features, shared feedback or supported Avenx.js in any way. Let's make the first stable release something worth remembering.

**See you on January 31, 2027.**
