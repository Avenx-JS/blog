Hacktoberfest has officially come to an end, and we're blown away by the contributions, bug fixes and improvements that came in from the community. Together, we've made Avenx.js better, faster and more robust.

## By the numbers

- **100** pull requests merged
- **100** issues closed

A huge shoutout to all our first-time contributors and returning maintainers alike. Your time, care and code mean the world to this project — and much of it lands right in time for the [road to v1.0.0](/avenx-v1-release-delayed).

## The door stays open

Hacktoberfest is over, but contributing isn't. Keep opening [issues](https://github.com/Avenx-JS/avenx-js/issues), joining [discussions](https://github.com/Avenx-JS/avenx-js/discussions) or sending pull requests year-round. You can find everyone who has contributed so far in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](https://github.com/Avenx-JS/avenx-js/blob/main/CONTRIBUTORS.md).

See you in the next release.
