The holiday season is just around the corner, and we're happy to share some news: **Avenx.js is taking part in 24 Pull Requests this December.**

If you haven't heard of it, [24 Pull Requests](https://24pullrequests.com/) is a yearly initiative that encourages developers to give something back to the open-source community throughout December. And what better way to celebrate the season than by building something together?

## Give a little back to open source

Whether you've followed Avenx.js for a while or have only just discovered it, December is a great time to get involved. You can:

- fix bugs and improve existing features
- help make the documentation even better
- work on new ideas and improvements
- make your very first contribution to Avenx.js
- meet other developers and become part of the community

Every contribution, no matter how small, makes a difference. Even a tiny improvement can make someone's experience with Avenx.js a little better.

## Join us

Avenx.js is listed on the official platform: [Avenx.js on 24 Pull Requests](https://24pullrequests.com/projects/4224). You can also explore [all our repositories on GitHub](https://github.com/Avenx-JS).

Looking for a place to start? Browse the issues labelled [`good first issue`](https://github.com/Avenx-JS/avenx-js/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22), [`help wanted`](https://github.com/Avenx-JS/avenx-js/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22help+wanted%22) or [`documentation`](https://github.com/Avenx-JS/avenx-js/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Adocumentation), and have a look at the [contributing guide](https://github.com/Avenx-JS/avenx-js/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) before you open a pull request.

## A little thank you

Avenx.js wouldn't be where it is today without everyone who has contributed their time, ideas, feedback and code — including the [100 pull requests merged during Hacktoberfest](/hacktoberfest-2026-wrap-up). We're grateful to everyone who has been part of this journey so far, and always excited to welcome new faces.

Let's make this December a little more special: share some knowledge, write some code and give something back to open source.

**Happy holidays, and happy contributing!**
